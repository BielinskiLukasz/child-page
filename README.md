# Spokojny Świat Artura

A calm, silent Polish-language web app for three-year-old Artur. A main menu with large picture tiles opens thirty-six gentle, touch-driven activities, each with a Back button. **Intended for supervised use by a young child.**

**Try it live: [Spokojny Świat Artura](https://bielinskilukasz.github.io/child-page/)**

## Activities
1. **Akwarium Artura** – a calm aquarium with fish. Touching the water drops food that the fish swim over to eat; touching a fish makes it blow soft bubbles.
2. **Magiczny Ogród** – a meadow with a random weather (sunny, cloudy, rain or breeze) and day or night each time it is opened. Touching the lawn grows a flower or a mushroom from a seed; touching the cloud gently clears the garden.
3. **Nocne Niebo** – glowing stars in a night sky. Touching them in turn connects the stars into a car, a bear, a house or a cat.
4. **Dzień i Noc** – touching the sun slowly brings night with stars, touching the moon brings the day back, touching the sky sends a cloud drifting by, a flock of birds crosses the daytime sky, and at night a steady white satellite or a plane with a pulsing red light sometimes drifts past.
5. **Ogród Motyli** – a garden of swaying flowers. Touching a flower makes the butterfly fly slowly to it along a smooth curve. Each visit picks a random set of flower colours and a random butterfly colouring (petal counts differ per flower, so colour is never the only cue).
6. **Malowanie Światłem** – a dark canvas. Touching and dragging paints soft glowing colours that drift and fade away after a few seconds.
7. **Mały Pociąg** – a little train with animals in its wagons. Touching the train starts or stops it, touching the station stops it there, and touching an animal makes it nod.
8. **Bańki Mydlane** – touching the screen blows a big soft bubble that floats up; touching a bubble splits it into smaller ones. A different painted background (one of 6) is chosen at random each time the activity is opened.
9. **Dobranoc** – a house at dusk. Touching a window dims or relights it and touching an animal tucks it in or wakes it; when all lights are off and all animals sleep, stars and a sleeping moon appear.
10. **Deszczyk i Tęcza** – touching a cloud makes it rain gently and the puddles ripple; each cloud rains on its own, and after a while of rain (faster when several clouds rain together) a soft rainbow appears and fades out again.
11. **Kolorowe Kształty** – soft shapes to drag onto their matching outlines (two grids: stacked in portrait, side by side in landscape). It starts with 2×2 shapes and, after three finished pictures, grows to 3×3; every new picture has fresh random shapes, colours (never repeated within one picture) and positions, and each launch starts again at 2×2. There are no wrong answers: a shape dropped elsewhere simply drifts back.
12. **Zwierzątka na Farmie** – a pastel farm. Touching an animal makes it react slowly; touching the barn opens its door so an animal can peek out.
13. **Policz Razem** – counting grows round by round: round 1 shows one friendly object (apples, ducks, flowers or fish, a different kind each round), round 2 shows two, up to ten; each touch adds a dot to a counting row and shows the numeral 1–10, the group glows softly when all are counted and the next round fades in. After 10 the sequence restarts at 1.
14. **Ubierz Misia** – a teddy bear in a room with a window. Touching the window slowly changes the weather (sun, rain, snow); six clothes (sun hat, umbrella, scarf, rain boots, warm hat, sunglasses) can be dragged onto the bear, which does a small happy hop with a soft glow for fitting clothes and a gentle head tilt for others; touching worn clothes takes them off.
15. **Przeciwieństwa** – four big objects (a door, a cup, a balloon, a lamp) that slowly change to their opposite when touched – open, full, high, on – and back again; the scene glows softly when all four are in their second state.
16. **Co nie Pasuje?** – three large pictures, two of one kind and one different (e.g. two fruits and a car). Touching the odd one makes it glow and bounce softly before the next set fades in; touching the others only makes them wobble, so there are no wrong answers.
17. **Literki** – one big letter (Aa) on a soft card with a friendly picture of something starting with it. Touching the picture makes it wiggle and shows its word for a few seconds, touching the letter makes it glow, and the arrows (or a swipe) move through all 32 Polish letters in a random order (each once per round, a new order every launch). Each showing picks a random one of at least two pictures per letter; ą, ę, ń and y have no native words starting with them, and ć and ó only one, so those pictures use words that merely contain the letter (labelled "w słowie").
18. **Ścieżka** – drag a finger along a wide, curvy path and a little figure (snail, car, ladybird or bee) slowly follows it to a flower or house; if the finger slips off, it simply waits. Arrow keys, Enter or Space also move it step by step.
19. **Wzorki** – a string of four beads in a simple repeating pattern with an empty slot; touching the matching one of two big beads glides it into place, the other one only wobbles gently, and after three beads the whole string glows and a new pattern appears.
20. **Zabawa w chowanego** – a garden with a bush, a box, a tree and a barrel. Touching a hiding place slowly lifts it to show the animal peeking behind it (or a flower if it is empty); when every place has been opened they glow softly, close and the animals move to new places.
21. **Koraliki** – a bowl of eight big colourful beads to drag onto a string; a bead released away from the string drifts back, and when all eight are threaded the necklace glows softly and the beads return to the bowl in a new order.
22. **Duże i Małe** – a big house and a small house, with big and small animals waiting at the bottom. Drag (or select) a big animal to the big house and a small one to the small house; anywhere else it floats gently back, and when all are home both houses glow and a new round with other animals begins.
23. **Kolorowe Kubeczki** – three pastel cups and six soft balls (two per colour) to drag into the cup of the same colour; colours also differ by a subtle pattern. A ball dropped elsewhere simply floats back, and when all are sorted the cups glow softly and the balls drift out in a new order.
24. **Puzzle** – a simple picture (a house with a sun, a fish or a flower) cut into four big pieces to drag to their places; pieces glide in when close and drift back otherwise. A finished picture glows softly and then fades into the next one.
25. **Wieża z Klocków** – six soft blocks to drag onto a tower base in any order; each settles on top with a tiny sway and the tower can never fall. When all six are stacked the top glows softly and the blocks drift back so the tower can be built again.
26. **Myjnia** – a muddy car (later a bus and an elephant) at a car wash. Dragging a finger scrubs the mud away and lets soap bubbles float up; when it is clean the vehicle glows softly and a new muddy one fades in.
27. **Cienie** – three grey animal shadows and the three matching coloured animals to drag onto them. An animal dropped on the wrong shadow simply floats back; when all are matched the picture glows softly and a new set of animals appears.
28. **Buźki** – a big friendly face and a small cat. Touching the face cycles calmly through happy, sleepy, surprised and calm expressions (the cat copies them); touching the eyes, cheeks or mouth gives a slow blink, a pink glow or a gentle smile or yawn.
29. **Pamięć** – six large picture cards (three pairs) to turn over two at a time. Matching cards glow and stay open, other cards simply turn back; when all pairs are found the cards glow and a new set of pictures is dealt.
30. **Gdzie jest Piłka?** – a ball, an open box on a table and a little bunny. Touching one of four marked spots (in, on, under, next to the box) or dragging the ball makes it glide there, and the bunny reacts gently.
31. **Rysuj Kształty** – tracing practice: a dotted line, circle, zigzag, cross, wave or spiral to follow with a finger. A soft, gently changing coloured line appears along the guide; when the shape is finished it glows and the next one fades in.
32. **Mały Samolot** – a little propeller plane with a bear pilot on a runway among hills and clouds. Touching the plane makes it take off and fly slowly, touching it again lands it; touching a cloud makes it curve around that cloud, and touching the pilot makes him wave.
33. **Traktor** – a pastel farm with a barn, a field of three rows and a little tractor with a bear driver. Touching the tractor starts or stops it (slowly), and it leaves sprouts that grow into flowers and vegetables; touching a plant makes it wiggle, touching the barn parks the tractor there, and touching the driver makes him wave. When all rows are full the plants fade and the field starts again.
34. **Autobusik** – a little bus drives slowly round a village road with three bus stops, each with a waiting animal. Touching the bus starts it or slows it to a halt; touching a stop makes it brake there (only the last touched stop counts), open its doors and let the animal hop in. Touching an animal in a window makes it step out at the stop or, while moving, wave.
35. **Rakieta** – a small rocket with a bear astronaut drifts through a starry night sky with three friendly planets. Touching the rocket makes it fly slowly along a smooth loop or slow down and hover; touching a planet makes it curve over, hover beside it while the planet glows softly, and then carry on. Touching a star makes it twinkle once, touching the astronaut makes him wave.
36. **Mała Łódka** – a small sailboat with a bear and a rabbit aboard floats slowly along a calm river with soft waves, a little jetty and three ducks. Touching the boat makes it sail or slow to a halt; touching the jetty makes it glide in and moor there (it never goes backwards). Touching a duck makes it nod and a soft ripple ring spread, touching an animal aboard makes it wave.

All activities are silent and calm, with no scores or failure, and work with touch, mouse or stylus. The Back button in the top-left corner returns to the menu. Parents can hide or reorder activities in Settings; see [Menu activities](#menu-activities).

## Run locally
Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step:

```
python3 -m http.server 8000
```
then open http://localhost:8000/. Navigation uses URL hashes and relative paths, so it works from any subpath.

## Install and offline use
The app is an installable PWA (manifest, icons, service worker); it needs HTTPS (GitHub Pages) or localhost.
- **Android Chrome:** open the site, menu ⋮ → **Zainstaluj aplikację** / *Install app*. It opens full-screen from the home-screen icon.
- **iPhone/iPad Safari:** Share → **Do ekranu początkowego** / *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Offline:** after one online load, the menu and all activities work in airplane mode. The service worker (`sw.js`) is network-first, so a new deployment is picked up on the next launch with network; no version bump is needed.
- **Old shortcut:** if a "shortcut" saved before this change misbehaves, remove it once (long-press the icon → remove/uninstall) and install again.
- Icons are generated by `python3 tools/make-icons.py`.

## Deployment
Pushing to `main` runs `.github/workflows/pages.yml`, which publishes to GitHub Pages. In the repo settings set Pages → Source to **GitHub Actions** once.

## Browser support
Mandatory manual tests: Chrome desktop, Chrome Android, Safari iPhone/iPad. Edge and Firefox should work. Zoom is intentionally disabled to avoid accidental pinch-zoom.

## Privacy
No backend, analytics, cookies, ads or tracking; no third-party requests, no data collected or transmitted. The only things stored, locally on the device (localStorage), are the parent-configured daily playtime limit, the playtime used today (with its date), and which activities are shown in the menu and in what order; it never leaves the device. No audio.

## Playtime limit
A gear icon on the main menu opens a parent gate (a simple sum) and then Settings: daily limit in minutes (default 20, or no limit) and a timer reset. The timer counts only visible activity time and resets each new day. An hourglass (no numbers) shows time left, content dims gently over the last 20%, and when time is up a sleeping-moon screen is shown.

## Menu activities
In Settings (behind the same gate) a parent can enable or disable each activity and reorder them with up/down buttons; "Przywróć domyślne" restores all of them in the original order. At least one activity always stays enabled, and disabled activities cannot be opened by any route (a stored or typed `#hash` shows the menu).

## Randomisation helper
`js/random.js` (loaded before the activities) exposes `window.Rand` for activity variety, kept in memory only (nothing stored, nothing scored): `Rand.shuffle(list)` returns a new shuffled copy; `Rand.pick(list, n)` returns `n` unique items (the whole pool shuffled if `n` exceeds it); `Rand.pickOne(list, key)` returns one item that differs from the previous result for the same `key` in this page session when the pool has 2+ entries. An empty pool gives `[]` / `undefined`.
